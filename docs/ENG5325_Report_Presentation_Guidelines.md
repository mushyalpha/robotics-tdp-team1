## Introduction

The final report and presentations for the team project should follow the guidelines given below. These describe, in general terms, the format and length of the design review presentation, the final report and the final presentation required for this team project. Although these provide a basic outline, they should not be taken as a definitive list of everything that is required for your submissions.

## Report Guidelines

### Structure

The report should cover the technical aspects of the project as well as results, analysis, team performance, and time and cost management. Overall, the narrative should be clear and concise. The basic structure of the report should include the following elements:

- **Abstract** – Brief summary of the report. Usually only half a page for a report of this type.
- **Introduction** – Outline of the project requirements in your own words.
- **Methodology** – Description of the implementation aspects for each stage of the assignment e.g. creation of model, validation, control system design, tests.
- **Results and Analysis** – Required results to illustrate completion of each stage and discussion of the analysis of these results.
- **Team Performance Analysis** – Evaluation of the performance of your team, including a comparison of your estimated and actual time/costs.
- **Conclusions and Further Work** – Findings from the methodology implementation and analysis of the results. Conclusions should end with an overall assessment of the project outcomes. Further work section should outline possible areas of improvement.
- **References** – List of documents used in this assignment.
- **Appendices** – Additional material not needed in the main sections of the report e.g. Matlab/ROS code, full Simulink/block diagrams.

Some additional elements/sections may be required. Each member of your team should contribute to the writing of the report. The report should indicate which team members were responsible for authoring the separate sections.

### Length of Report

The report should be **30 pages** in length. The references and appendices are not included in the page count. Do not exceed this number of pages as you may be penalised.

### Presentation, Style and Structure

In addition to the marks awarded for the technical aspects of the project, marks are awarded to the presentation, writing style and structure of your report.

**Font:** The size of font used for main text is typically 10–12pt, and titles 12–16pt.

**Captions and Labelling:** All figures, tables and equations must be numbered appropriately. Figures and tables need descriptive captions. Axes on graphs must be labelled appropriately.

### Report Submission

Your team's report should be in Word or PDF format and submitted through the Moodle submission portal.

## Final Presentation Guidelines

### Length

The presentation should be **20 minutes** long (15 minutes for the presentation and 5 minutes for the video demonstration), plus 5 minutes for questions — making a total of 25 minutes.

As a guide, aim for roughly the same number of slides as minutes to present (i.e. 15–20 slides). This is a general rule of thumb only.

### Structure

The final presentation should follow the same basic structure as your report, condensed to fit the duration. Use the following as a guide:

- **Introduction** – Outline of the project requirements and presentation structure. Introduce your team and indicate which member will be presenting each subject area.
- **Methodology** – Description of the implementation aspects for each stage of the project e.g. creation of model, validation, control system design, tests.
- **Results and Analysis** – Required results to illustrate completion of each stage and discussion of the analysis of these results.
- **Team Performance Analysis** – Evaluation of the performance of your team, including a comparison of estimated and actual time/costs.
- **Conclusions** – Findings from the study as a bullet point list. Overall assessment of the project outcomes.
- **Demonstration** – Video demonstration of the key elements of the simulation working. This should be presented after the main presentation.

**Font:** Use approximately 20pt font to ensure readability. Use bullet points and avoid long paragraphs or blocks of text.

### Team Contributions

Each member of your team should contribute to both the creation and the delivery of the presentation.

### Presentation Submission

Your team's presentation should be in PowerPoint format and submitted through the Moodle submission portal.

> **Submission window:** 9:00 am on 23rd March to 4:30 pm on 27th March
